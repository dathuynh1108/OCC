## What was checked for this slide update

- Imported source identities and row coverage: 10 summary rows, 150
  category–method rows, 45 paired category–seed rows.
- Equality of category-average AUROC means and the published summary means.
- Paired AUROC deltas and their sample SD from three seed macro means.
- Compiled PDF and visual inspection of all slide layouts.

The original dataset and fitted GPU checkpoints were not available in this
slide-editing task. Training, prediction-level auditing and full CNN replay
were **not** repeated here. Their published status is reported with attribution.

## Figures

The earlier accepted deck supplies 14 explanatory figures. They are analytic
schematics/toy geometry, not fitted MVTec representations. Their labels remain
schematic, and their Python generator is included.

Four new, separate Matplotlib plots are generated from the imported CSVs:
`mvtec_macro_auroc`, `mvtec_ablations`, `mvtec_categories`, `mvtec_paired_deltas`.
The full-scale heatmap includes all 15 categories and all ten methods. AUROC
axes and error-bar meanings are explicit. The additional paired-delta plot is
included as a reusable figure; its numbers also appear on the results slide.

The original vector-pilot result plots and numbers are not used as MVTec evidence.
No alternative ablation or seed replaces the declared final NBD variant.

## Editorial separation in this revision

The baseline and NBD sections are separated without changing any measured value.
The early comparison uses only the four already-explained baseline methods.
The later comparison adds NBD and its geometric-byte-budget PatchScore control;
all ten measured variants remain in the appendix and all-category figure.

`PatchScore (128 centers)` maps exactly to `PatchScore_same_centers`.
No alternative seed, threshold, split or best ablation is substituted.
The common image-threshold calibration stays in the baseline setup, while
the distinct component normal-tail scaling is explained only in the NBD section.

Two extra result figures, `mvtec_baseline_auroc` and `mvtec_nbd_comparison`,
are generated by `code/make_comparison_figures.py` from the same summary CSV.
The figure-view manifest lists the exact CSV keys used by each plot.
